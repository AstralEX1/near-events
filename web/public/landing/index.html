The problem

Clicking “Going” is free. So nobody means it.

Open the door and you run out of food. Gate it with tickets and you lose the room to a free meetup next door — while your “full” list is people who never meant to come.

RSVPs are pretty meaningless

People say yes to five free events and pick one that evening. Half the list never walks in.

Food and rooms still cost money

Free for guests isn’t free for you. You budget for 60, thirty show — or the hall looks full online and serious people can’t get a seat.

Tickets scare people off

Charge $10 and your crowd goes to the free meetup across the street. A deposit isn’t a ticket — it’s skin in the game.

How it works

Add a little friction.

To RSVP to a free event, you put down a deposit. Show up and you get it back. No-show — it’s taken and split among who came.

Why NEAR

NEAR handles the money.

→

Any coin, any chain

Guests pay with what they already hold, like ETH on Base, SOL or USDT on Tron. NEAR Intents turns it into USDC on the way in, and pays winners out to whatever chain they want.

escrow contract

A contract holds the pool

Deposits sit in an escrow contract on NEAR, not in our bank account. It pays back only the people who checked in and splits the rest between them. Anyone can read the rules.

Sign in with Google$0 gas

No wallet, no gas

Guests sign in with Google or email and get a NEAR account behind the scenes. We cover the network fee, so the deposit is the only thing they pay.

Frequently asked questions

Will guests need a crypto wallet?

No. Guests can sign in with Google or email. People who have a NEAR wallet can use it too.

What blockchain is this on?

NEAR. Guests pay in USDC from any chain, and NEAR Intents moves it to NEAR for them.

Can I charge for tickets?

Yes. Choose Ticket instead of Stake to RSVP. The money goes to you when you close the event.

What happens to no-show deposits?

Guests who were checked in at the door share them. No platform fee.
