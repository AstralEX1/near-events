How it works

Add a little friction.

Ask guests for a small refundable deposit. Here's what happens to it.

1

Everyone deposits a small amount when they RSVP

The host picks the amount, usually around 10 USDC. You get it back when you check in. Pay from any chain or sign in with Google.
